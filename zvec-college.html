Zambia Vocational & Entrepreneurship College

ZVEC College

Learn. Build. Grow.

ZVEC College is the flagship of ZVEC Group. It started as Cake Market College and has trained 90,000 students in the past 5 years across Zambia. We are licensed by the Government of Zambia through TEVETA to provide vocational and entrepreneurship training.
