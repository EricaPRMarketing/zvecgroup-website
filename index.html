Our story

Built to empower.

What began as Cake Market College, a small culinary school, has grown into a proudly Zambian-owned group that creates jobs, backs entrepreneurs and gives women and young people the skills and tools to earn a living.

Our vision

To be Zambia's leading empowerment-driven business group, transforming lives through education, entrepreneurship, innovation and quality service.

Our mission

To provide affordable, accessible and innovative solutions that create opportunities, improve livelihoods and contribute to national development.
